Almost every houseplant dies of kindness

Overwatering outnumbers every other cause of death we see, by a long way. A plant that is thirsty tells you within a day and recovers within two. A plant sitting in wet compost tells you nothing for three weeks and then cannot be saved.

Push a finger in to the second knuckle. If it comes out with compost stuck to it, wait. That single habit will keep more plants alive than any feed or gadget.
